# 🍃 Leafora Tea Branding

> **Premium Ceylon Tea Branding & Packaging Design**  
> University Group Project

---

## 📖 Project Overview

Leafora is a premium Ceylon tea branding project developed as part of a university group assignment. The objective of the project was to create a modern tea brand identity that reflects the elegance and authenticity of Sri Lankan Ceylon tea while appealing to contemporary consumers through premium packaging and visual communication.

The brand combines luxury aesthetics with natural elements, using a rich green color palette, elegant gold typography and botanical illustrations to communicate freshness, quality and sophistication.

# 💡 Brand Story

The name **Leafora** combines the words **Leaf** and **Aura**, symbolizing the natural beauty, freshness and calming experience of premium Ceylon tea.

The visual identity was carefully crafted to create a luxurious yet organic appearance through elegant typography, gold accents and botanical elements inspired by nature.
